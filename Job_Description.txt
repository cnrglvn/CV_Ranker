JOB TITLE: Software Engineer
Department: Product Engineering
Reports to: Engineering Manager
Location: Hybrid (3 days on-site)

About the Role

We are seeking a Software Engineer to join our product engineering team, contributing to the design, development, and maintenance of scalable web applications. This role involves close collaboration with product managers, designers, and fellow engineers to deliver reliable, well-tested software.

Key Responsibilities

Design, develop, and maintain backend and frontend features across our core product
Write clean, well-documented, and thoroughly tested code
Participate in code reviews, providing and receiving constructive feedback
Collaborate with product and design teams to translate requirements into technical solutions
Diagnose and resolve production issues in a timely manner
Contribute to architectural decisions and technical planning
Participate in an on-call rotation to support system reliability

Requirements

3+ years of professional software development experience
Proficiency in at least one modern programming language (e.g. Python, JavaScript, Go)
Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design principles
Experience with version control systems (e.g. Git) and collaborative development workflows
Familiarity with relational or NoSQL databases
Strong written and verbal communication skills

Nice to Have

Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and containerization tools
Prior experience working in an agile development environment

What We Offer

Competitive salary and equity package
Comprehensive health, dental, and vision coverage
Flexible paid time off
Professional development budget
Collaborative, supportive engineering culture

We are an equal opportunity employer and welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds.
