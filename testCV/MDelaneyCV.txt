Marcus Delaney brings twelve years of civil engineering expertise, specializing in sustainable infrastructure and urban water management systems. Throughout his career, he has led design teams on municipal projects ranging from stormwater retrofits to greenway corridor construction, consistently delivering under budget and ahead of schedule. Marcus holds a Chartered Engineer designation and has published two technical papers on low-impact development strategies. He is skilled in AutoCAD, Civil 3D, and hydraulic modeling software, and has served as a technical reviewer for regional planning boards. Known for clear communication with non-technical stakeholders, he regularly presents at community planning meetings.
